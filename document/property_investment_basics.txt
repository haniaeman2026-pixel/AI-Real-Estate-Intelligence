REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT BASICS

Real estate investment involves purchasing or holding property with the expectation of receiving financial benefits through rental income, appreciation, or other uses.

IMPORTANT INVESTMENT FACTORS

Location:
Location can influence demand, rental opportunities, accessibility, and property values.

Purchase Price:
The purchase price should be compared with expected income, expenses, and comparable properties.

Rental Potential:
For rental properties, potential rental income should be compared with operating and ownership costs.

Operating Costs:
Costs can include maintenance, repairs, utilities, taxes, insurance, management fees, and other property-related expenses.

Property Condition:
A property requiring major repairs may have additional costs beyond its purchase price.

Market Conditions:
Property markets can change over time. Historical performance does not guarantee future results.

Cash Flow:
Investors may analyze expected rental income against recurring expenses and financing costs.

Long-Term Planning:
Investment decisions should consider the intended holding period, financial objectives, risk tolerance, and future plans.

RISK

Real estate investments involve financial risk. Property values can decline, rental demand can change, expenses can increase, and properties may take time to sell.

Investors should verify financial assumptions and obtain appropriate professional advice before making major financial decisions.
